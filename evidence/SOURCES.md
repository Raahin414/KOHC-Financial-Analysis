# Official-source evidence

The unchanged Audit workbook is the detailed source record, including annual-report references, exact PSX endpoints, raw trading rows, response hashes, share bases, corporate actions and historical forensic findings. CSV files here are read-only exports from the named Audit sheets. Saved PSX HTML responses retain all original response bytes. They are historical evidence, not fresh retrievals made for this repository import.

Official report portals:

- KOHC: https://www.kohatcement.com/financial_reports.aspx
- LUCK: https://www.lucky-cement.com/investor-relations/downloads/financial-reports/
- DGKC: https://www.dgcement.com/financial-reports/DGAnnual2025.pdf
- MLCF: https://www.kmlg.com/mlcfl/financials/
- FCCL: https://fccl.com.pk/eng/financial-reports/
- PSX: https://www.psx.com.pk/ and https://dps.psx.com.pk/

Corporate-action dates must be read from the retained evidence, not inferred from per-share changes. Official annual reports are linked rather than duplicated in full. Secondary investigative sources retained inside the Audit are not authoritative financial inputs.
