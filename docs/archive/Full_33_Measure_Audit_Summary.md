# Full financial audit and reconciliation — 33 measures

Completed 22 October 2026. Scope: KOHC, LUCK, DGKC, MLCF and FCCL standalone financial analysis for FY2021–FY2025, with relevant FY2020 opening balances. This revision completes the non-market review and preserves the earlier official PSX verification.

The submission and audit workbooks contain exactly **33 measures: 19 mandatory guideline ratios and 14 additional measures**. Net Debt/EBITDA, EBITDA Margin and Operating Cash Flow Ratio are removed from headline company tables, sample averages and charts. EBITDA remains only in the internal audit where needed to test a published interest-coverage convention.

**Result and limits.** All 825 standardized outputs (805 numeric results and 20 project-policy `n/m` results) and all 165 sample averages matched independent recomputation, saved workbook values and Microsoft Excel 16.0 recalculation. This verifies the standardized implementation. It does not establish that every company-published ratio is consistent with its own statements. Official-source discrepancies remain explicitly open below. No figures were hardcoded to force agreement.

## Consistent methods and project presentation

The four prioritized mandatory ratios were reviewed at numerator and denominator level:

- **Inventory turnover:** COGS / average total stores and stock across all companies. Published sales/closing-inventory or stock-only conventions are reconstructed separately.
- **Quick ratio:** (current assets − total stores and stock) / all current liabilities. DGKC’s narrower quick assets and FCCL’s parent-loan exclusion are not imposed on the standardized formula.
- **Debt-to-equity:** total liabilities / total equity. Published long-term bank financing/equity or gearing percentages are separate tests.
- **Times interest earned:** EBIT / expensed interest, excluding bank charges and foreign-exchange items from the interest denominator. EBIT is pre-levy profit before tax plus all finance cost; core operating margin excludes other income. Published EBITDA, operating-numerator and bank-interest-only conventions are separate tests.

Cash ratio retains the project’s narrow cash-and-bank scope, excluding short-term investments. Debt-to-capital uses interest-bearing principal plus leases, excluding interest-free parent funding and accrued markup. Long-term debt means non-current interest-bearing financing and leases. These boundaries are explicit because other textbook and company conventions can produce different results.

The project’s `n/m` policy applies to negative calculated measures and non-positive denominators. Negative accounting inputs, losses, tax effects and raw calculations remain signed in source statements and the audit. Negative percentage-growth outputs follow the same policy; absolute movements and common-size signed expenses remain. Zero is numeric zero, sometimes displayed as a dash by the existing format. Sample averages include available numeric observations and exclude text `n/m`, using embedded formulas.

The ZIP contains project guidelines and the sample project, but no separate class-note formula file or written TA correspondence was identifiable. The retained 33-measure selection and existing standardized class-style formulas were therefore applied consistently and documented. This audit does **not** claim independent confirmation of an unavailable TA instruction. Official accounting adjustments correct input classification without changing formulas to reproduce published values.
